Frequently asked

A few of the questions we hear most often before a first engagement.

What channels do you actually cover?

National and regional print and online press, trade and specialist outlets, TV and radio broadcasts, podcasts, and the public layer of major social platforms. Coverage scope is tuned to your sector and language footprint at the scoping stage.

How fast are alerts delivered?

For online and social mentions, alerts typically land within minutes of publication. Broadcast and print are processed as transcripts/clippings become available. Crisis-tier engagements include a named analyst on call for prioritised triage.

Is everything you do strictly legal?

Yes. We work exclusively with public sources, licensed media archives and consent-based access. We never scrape private accounts, bypass paywalls or break the GDPR. If a request would cross a line, we say so up front and offer a lawful alternative.

How confidential is the engagement?

All engagements run under a signed NDA. Files are stored encrypted, communications happen over end-to-end-encrypted channels by default, and only the analysts assigned to your account have access. We do not subcontract abroad without your written approval.

How are you priced?

Ongoing monitoring runs on a fixed monthly retainer scaled to channels, languages and alert volume. One-off reputation audits and crisis support are scoped as fixed-fee engagements. You always receive a written scope and price before work begins.

Need eyes on the story?

Tell us what you need to see — coverage, sentiment, a brewing crisis. The first 30-minute consultation is free, fully confidential, and comes with no obligation to engage.
