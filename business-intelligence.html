Frequently asked

A few of the questions we hear most often before a first engagement.

How is business intelligence different from market research?

Market research typically answers a one-off question with a survey or a report. Business intelligence is continuous, actionable and decision-led — we combine open-source research, lawful human intelligence and structured analysis so the output directly supports a strategic call you have to make.

Is everything you do strictly legal?

Yes. We work exclusively with open sources, lawful human intelligence and consent-based technical access. We never hack accounts, intercept communications without authorisation, or break the GDPR. If a request would cross a line, we say so up front and offer a lawful alternative.

How confidential is the engagement?

All engagements run under a signed NDA. Files are stored encrypted, communications happen over end-to-end-encrypted channels by default, and only the analysts assigned to your case have access. We do not subcontract abroad without your written approval.

How are you priced?

Most engagements are scoped as a fixed-fee package after a free 30-minute consultation. Ongoing monitoring or retainers are billed monthly. You always receive a written scope and price before any analytical work begins.

How long does a typical engagement take?

A focused market or competitor study typically lands in 2–4 weeks. Continuous monitoring runs on a monthly cadence. Complex multi-market work can take longer — we give you a realistic timeline at the scoping stage.

Have a question in mind?

Tell us what you need to understand. The first 30-minute consultation is free, fully confidential, and comes with no obligation to engage.
