Frequently asked

A few of the questions we hear most often before a first engagement.

Is everything you do strictly legal?

Yes. We work exclusively with open sources, lawful human intelligence and consent-based technical access. We never hack accounts, intercept communications without authorisation, or break the GDPR. If a request would cross a line, we say so up front and offer a lawful alternative.

How confidential is the engagement?

All engagements run under a signed NDA. Files are stored encrypted, communications happen over end-to-end-encrypted channels by default, and only the analysts assigned to your case have access. We do not subcontract abroad without your written approval.

How are you priced?

Most cases are scoped as a fixed-fee package after a free 30-minute consultation. Open-ended monitoring or retainers are billed monthly. You always receive a written scope and price before any investigative work begins.

How long does an investigation take?

A focused OSINT report typically lands in 5–10 working days. Counter-surveillance sweeps are usually scheduled within a week. Complex multi-jurisdiction work can take several weeks - we give you a realistic timeline at the scoping stage.

Will the report hold up in court?

Yes. Reports are written to evidence standards, with full source citations, hashes for digital exhibits and a chain-of-custody log. We can attend as an expert witness and coordinate directly with your legal counsel where needed.

Have a case in mind?

Tell us what you are dealing with. The first 30-minute consultation is free, fully confidential, and comes with no obligation to engage.
